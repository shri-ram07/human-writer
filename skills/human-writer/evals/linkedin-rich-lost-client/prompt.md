Write a LinkedIn post for me. I'm Kavya Iyer, co-founder of Tilt Analytics, a 9-person data consultancy in Bengaluru.

What happened: in August 2026 we lost our biggest client, a D2C skincare brand that was 38% of our revenue. They moved analytics in-house after hiring a head of data. We saw it coming in May, when they stopped inviting us to their planning calls, and we did nothing about it.

What we changed afterwards: no single client above 20% of revenue; a monthly "are we still useful to you" call with every client; and a fixed-price GA4 audit, which brought in 6 new clients in 8 weeks.

I don't want it to read like a humblebrag or a 'lessons learned' listicle. Keep it under 250 words.
